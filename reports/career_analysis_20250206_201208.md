# Career Analysis Report

## Career Direction and Primary Recommendations

### **Career Paths**

* **Software Engineer**
	+ Role Description: Design, develop, and test software applications to meet the needs of organizations.
	+ Why it's a good fit:
		- Exciting work with a sense of problem-solving
		- Opportunities for career growth and specialization
	+ Potential Positions and Companies:
		- Entry-level positions in development teams
		- Mid-level positions in product management or engineering leadership
* **Data Analyst**
	+ Role Description: Collect, analyze, and interpret complex data to inform business decisions.
	+ Why it's a good fit:
		- High demand for skilled professionals in the field of data analysis
		- Opportunities for career advancement in various industries
	+ Potential Positions and Companies:
		- Entry-level positions in data analysis teams
		- Mid-level positions in statistical modeling or business intelligence

### **Additional Recommendations**

* Consider earning a degree in computer science, statistics, or data science to enhance job prospects.
* Develop skills in programming languages such as Python, Java, or C++.
